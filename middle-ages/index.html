Middle Ages Explained

Middle Ages Explained is a YouTube channel of short animated stories about medieval history: daily life, myths and reality, weapons and castles, dark curiosities and famous events, built on historical sources.

About the app

"historia-shorts" is a private tool used only by the owner of the channel to upload, schedule and manage the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API. It has no other users and offers no sign-in to the public.
