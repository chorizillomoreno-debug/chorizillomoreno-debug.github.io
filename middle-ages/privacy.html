Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

This policy explains how the "historia-shorts" application handles data obtained through the YouTube API Services and Google APIs.

Who uses it

The application is used exclusively by the owner of the Middle Ages Explained channel to manage that channel. It does not offer accounts or sign-in to anyone else.

Data accessed

  • Uploading, editing, scheduling and deleting videos on the owner's own channel.
  • Basic information about those videos (title, status, publication time) and aggregate statistics of the channel's own videos.

How data is used

Data is used only to publish the channel's videos at their scheduled time and to improve future videos. It is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

Storage

Access credentials are stored encrypted as private secrets of the owner. No data from other YouTube users is collected.

Google services

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy also apply.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions. When revoked, any stored data is deleted within 30 days.
